## Product Strategy Canvas

### 1. Vision
"Ayla — AI-компаньон по качеству жизни"
Beauty booking as entry point → daily wellness companion → lifestyle platform

### 2. Target Market
- Primary: Women 20-45, Russian cities 500K+ population
- Pilot: Penza
- Expansion: Kazan → Moscow/SPb → Kazakhstan (Phase 5)
- TAM/SAM/SOM analysis needed

### 3. Competitive Moat
| Layer | Moat | Defensibility |
|-------|------|---------------|
| AI Memory | Remembers preferences, history, patterns | High — data flywheel |
| Daily Habit | Food Scanner → wellness → booking | Medium — switching cost |
| Two-sided Network | Specialists + Clients | High — marketplace effect |
| Brand (Ayla) | Warm AI friend, not cold tool | Low — easily copied |

### 4. Business Model
- 8% commission on bookings (current)
- Premium subscription (future): advanced AI, priority booking
- Specialist tools (future): analytics, CRM, marketing

### 5. Key Risks
- Cold start problem (two-sided marketplace)
- AI trust gap (will users trust AI for beauty choices?)
- Regulatory (152-ФЗ, health claims for nutrition features)
- Competition from incumbents (Yclients ecosystem)

### 6. Strategic Bets
- Bet 1: AI-first UX will drive adoption over traditional search
- Bet 2: Food Scanner creates daily engagement beauty apps lack
- Bet 3: Memory/personalization creates switching costs
